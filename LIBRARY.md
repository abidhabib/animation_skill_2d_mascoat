# Scene library cheat sheet (see LIBRARY_CATALOG.png for pictures; test clip: specs/clip100.json)

Every scene is `{"name","start","dur","type":"stage","bg":...}`; times are scene-local seconds (or voice-over phrases for start/end/say.at).

## Environments (`bg`)
street (`time` day|dusk|night), railway, harbor, dayCloud, buildSite, neonNight, officeNight, office1925, serverHall,
bedroom / kitchen / classroom / hospital / livingRoom (`time` day|night), factory, lab, stadium.

## Props (`props:[{type,x,y,scale,dir,move:[t0,t1,dx],col,label,front}]`, y = ground line)
Vehicles: car (`beam:true` headlights), bus (`label`), truck (`label`), train (smoke), plane, bike, boat (`sail:true`). Wheels rotate with the move distance.
Machines: factoryLine (moving boxes), robotArm (swinging), serverRack (blinking), labBench (bubbling), controlPanel (blinking).

## People
Hero + cast: `chars:[{name,style:"mascot",x,y,scale,view,dir,walk:[t0,t1,dx],shirt,skin,glasses,curl}]`
Crowd: `"crowd":{"n":22,"x0":0,"x1":1900,"y":820,"scale":0.8,"walk":[0,2],"seed":9,"still":false}` (varied mascots, mixed directions, never yellow).
Group: `"group":{"n":5,"x":110,"dx":135,"y":1015,"hats":["bowler"],"walk":[0,2,500]}`.
Talking: `"say":[{"char":"hero","emotion":"crying","at":"spoken phrase"}]`.
Semi-3D: `"vol":true` on a mascot (form shading, specular highlights, rim lights, chin/ground shadows; see docs/SEMI3D_BEFORE_AFTER.png). Head turn: `"turns":[[t,dur,yaw,fromYaw]]` with yaw -1 (left) .. 0 (front) .. 1 (right), see docs/HEAD_TURN.png; or call `T.turn(tl,id,t,dur,yaw)`.
Aging: `"age":0..70` on a mascot (15 first lines, 30 glasses + grey tufts, 50 grey brows + spots, 55+ cane, 60+ white hair, side/three-quarter views stoop). `cane:true/false` overrides. See docs/MASCOT_AGES.png.

## Thumbnails (`python3 thumb.py specs/thumb_foo.json name` -> thumbnails/name.png 1920x1080 + name_1280x720.jpg)
Spec = one scene with `"type":"thumb"`: `bg` (any env, or `white`), `text` (`["TOO","SLOW."]` or "TOO SLOW."; last word yellow, `accent` recolours, `textPos` bl|br|tl|tr, `textSize`, `textRot`), `badge` ("10x", `badgeCol`, `badgeSize`, `badgeX/Y`), `lines` (speed lines `{dir:"left|right",col,n}`), `glow` (`{x,y,r,col}`), `burst` (impact star), `arrow` (`{x,y,rot,k,col}`), `props` (vehicles etc, `rot` tilts a prop), `chars` with `pose` (`run punch float cross point shock stand`; mascots get `vol:true` shading automatically) and `say` for the face (`smug`, `shocked`...). Examples: `specs/thumb_example_speed.json`, `specs/thumb_example_white.json`; pictures `docs/THUMB_EXAMPLE_*.jpg`. Thumbnail rules: `PACKAGING_PLAYBOOK.md` (2-word reaction text that is NOT the title, aftermath not the power, number badge, alternate dark neon and white backgrounds).

## YouTube Shorts, portrait 9:16 (built 2026-11-19; every long video gets one Short)
`bash new-clip.sh S1` makes **Short 1** (stored as clip 90; S2 = clip 91 ...): pairs the files named `Short 1 - Voice-over.mp3` (name must contain "Short" + the number) with `Short 1 - ....txt` in `Voice-overs Chunks/` and `Transcript Chunks/`, writes `meta.json` with `"portrait": true` and a 1080x1920 `index.html`. Then `python3 scene.py 90 specs/short1.json` and `bash render-clip.sh S1` -> `renders/Short 1.mp4`. The same scene specs and kit work: the scene is cropped to a 607.5x1080 slice of the 1920x1080 stage (x 656..1264, centred on x=960, scaled 1.78x; vector art stays sharp). **Keep the hero, number badges and text inside x 690..1230**; aim the camera with `cam:{fx:<hero x>}` to follow someone elsewhere. Hero `scale` about 1.8-2.4 fills the frame; captions in the top third (y < 400) or bottom third; leave y > 1700 clear of text (YouTube UI). Tested: 1080x1920 render of a street + hero + number badge (frame checked). No voice-over yet? `SILENT=45 bash new-clip.sh S1` makes a silent 45 s project (hand-set scene times, add the voice later). Portrait text rules (learned from Short 1): `pop` items must fit about 540 px: font size about 940 / number of characters (12 letters -> fs 70), split long lines; captions on a dark rounded pill (`rect` fill #0b0620 opacity .6) so they stay readable; hero scale 1.75 at y 1040 leaves room above the head for a caption (y 60-230) AND a number tag. Working example: `specs/short_trust.json` (8 scenes, 45 s). **Layout rules (user, 2026-11-19):** centre text blocks vertically (equal space above and below, never pushed to the bottom: for the 1080-tall stage a block's centre is y about 540); every side character must LOOK like its role and age, never a recoloured copy of the hero: use `style:"pear"` with different `hair`, `hairCol`, `skin`, `shirt`, `suit`+`tie`, `stache`, `glasses` (dad = grey hair + stache + glasses + cardigan colour, boss = suit + tie, friend = other skin tone and hair), and only the hero is the yellow mascot (aged hero: `age`). Cast test sheet: `thumbnails/castcheck_1280x720.jpg`.

## Long-video pieces (built for V02 clips 1-2, 2026-11-19)
- `"phone":{x,y,k,screen:"lock"|"call",time,date,name,sub}` on a stage scene: a phone held in a hand; characters placed in the screen are clipped to it (a photo, a video call) and their tags glow on the screen. Groups `#<sid>-phb/-phc/-phf` (+ `#<sid>-tgw0`) shake together. Put the character low (e.g. role mom, y 1065, scale 1.5) so the tag sits under the lock-screen clock.
- `"bed":{ch:"hero",col,phone:{t0,t1}}` with `bg:"white"`: top-down "in bed" shot (sheet, pillow, blanket up to the chin, phone buzzing beside the pillow, `#<sid>-bph` can fly to the camera).
- Prop `toaster` (burnt toast + rising smoke, `burnt:false`, `smoke:false`).
- Hands in front of the face (rubbing eyes, covering the mouth): the real arms are drawn behind the head, so set `armL:186, armR:-186` and draw sleeve + fist shapes in `extra` (example: V02 `specs/make_clip01_02.py`, scene s4-rub).
- No `fg` foreground silhouettes indoors (they read as black blobs); keep them for streets.
- Voice per clip: `python3 "../_Studio Kit/fishjoin.py" "Clip 02" "<text>" <fish-url-or-file> ... [gap=0.5] ...` joins Fish parts (Elliot 1.2x lines + other voices) into the clip voice-over + transcript.
- **Specs follow the words**: write the clip spec as a small generator that reads `clips/clip-NN/assets/vo.words.json` and places every scene cut, number pop, text slam and camera move on a spoken word (`Words(n).t('phrase')`), never on hand-typed seconds. A re-recorded voice then re-times everything with one run. Example: V02 `specs/make_clip01_02.py`.
- Clip rhythm that the user asked for: 4-5 shots per 15-17 s clip, a new picture on every sentence, camera always moving (push, pull, track, punch on numbers), whip or zoom-through between shots.

## KIT V2 (2026-11-19): 2.5D characters, cast roles, cinematic environments. Pictures: docs/CAST_ROLES.png, docs/KIT_V2_SHOTS.png, test `renders/Kit v2 test.mp4`, spec `specs/kit_v2_test.json`
Old kit is saved: git tag `kit-v1-before-opus-upgrade` + folder `../Backups/kit-v1-2026-11-19`.
**2.5D on every mascot by default** (`vol:false` for flat): volume shading, far arm/leg darker (side and 3/4 views), head leads the body a little when the camera pans, curl + hoodie strings swing (stronger while walking), weight shift + breathing when standing, gestures have anticipation + overshoot.
- Light that reacts to the scene: scene `"light":{"rim":"#ff5fd2","fill":"#7ae0ff","side":"right"}` (flipped characters are handled), or per char `rim`, `fill`, `lightSide`.
- Body turn: `"spin":[[t,"right"],[t2,"front"]]` (front|right|left|q3r|q3l|back); front<->side passes through a 3/4 frame. Talk/tags target the character's FIRST view (start in the view it talks in).
- Lip sync from the voice: `new-clip.sh` runs `lipsync.py` -> `assets/vo.env.json`; every `say` window then opens the mouth closed/open/wide from the real loudness (falls back to random flaps without audio).
**Cast** (`"role":"dad"`; any key you set wins): dad, mom, grandpa, grandma, boss, kid, student, cashier, doctor, teacher, worker, chef, police, friend, roommate. Build your own with: `hair` short|side|spiky|curly|long|bun|ponytail|bald + `hairCol`; `hat` cap|beanie|hardhat|chef|bowler|cop + `hatCol`; `outfit` hoodie|tee|shirt|suit|coat|apron|vest|cardigan|uniform|dress (+ `tie`, `inner`, `apron`); `skirt`, `scarf`, `backpack` (colours); `stache`, `beard`, `glasses`, `age`, `skin`, `shirt`, `pants`; `build` slim|wide|kid. Only the hero keeps the cream head + curl + yellow hoodie. Crowds now mix hair/outfits/hats/builds/ages automatically.
**Depth**: characters are drawn in y order (lower on screen = in front); `depth:true` (auto in alley/rooftop) scales them by distance; `depthSort:false` to turn off. Use names (not numbers) in `say`/`tags`.
**Environments**: real camera parallax on every rich bg (far 0.35, mid 0.78, also when zooming; `parallax:[far,mid]`), `fg:true` blurred foreground silhouettes passing at 1.7x, `dof:2` background blur, `life:{n:10}` far pedestrians + cars crossing, `timeShift:{t,dur,to:"night"}` day->dusk->night inside one shot (windows light up), `weather:"rain"` + `weatherAt:t` fades it in, wet-ground reflections automatic with rain (`reflect:true/false`). New portrait-composed bgs: `alley` (perspective lane to a lit street, windows, fire escape, string lights, neon sign, puddles, bins; day|dusk|night) and `rooftop` (skyline, water tower, AC unit, string lights; day|dusk|night).
**Camera presets** (one line): `"shots":[{"type":"track","on":"hero"}]` (follows the walk, feet at 80%), `{"type":"push","on":"hero","t":0.5,"dur":4,"k":1.85}` (slow push to the face), `reveal`, `orbit` (`amp`), `punch`; handheld is on by default for `stage` scenes (`handheld:false` to stop); exits `"out":{"kind":"whip"|"zoom"|"fade","dir":1}` pair with the next scene's `"in"`.
**User rules from Short 1 review (2026-11-19)**: (1) every narration beat gets its OWN picture that shows what the line says (cancelled plans = "Can't make it" bubbles + the friend's number dropping; forgot $200 = a bill flying away + the number dropping); never fill a beat with a character walking aimlessly. (2) No weather/rain unless the story calls for it. (3) Vary the places (street, kitchen, office, living room, alley, bedroom, rooftop) so a Short never feels like one scene. (4) Transitions: zoom-through (`"out":{"kind":"zoom","at":[x,y]}` dives into a point such as a face or a number, the next scene's `"in":{"kind":"zoom"}` pulls out of it), alternated with whips; `at` is in stage coordinates and is converted for portrait automatically. (5) Call-to-action lines ("Full story on my channel") are never sped up (fishvo.py keeps them at 1.0x).
**No brand marks by default**: `officeNight` only shows the Video 1 brand mark + four-colour stripe with `"brand":true`. **First real kit v2 Short**: `specs/short1_v2.json` (7 shots, 41.9 s, Fish voice-over, lip sync) -> `renders/Short 1.mp4`; push close-ups use `dy:-110` so the number over the head stays below the top 12%.
**9:16 safe zone by default**: Shorts (`new-clip.sh S<k>`) lift the camera 160 px (`lift` to change) so feet at y 1040 land about 80% down; `scene.py` prints `SAFE-ZONE:` warnings for characters/text outside the box; `safeGuide:true` draws the box for previews.

## Vertical safe zone (user complaint 2026-11-19: character at the very bottom of the screen looks weird)
Shorts/Reels UI covers about the bottom 20% (title, channel, buttons), the top 12% and the right 15%. In the portrait slice the stage is 1080 tall: keep feet at stage y about 860-880 (not 1040) by setting the scene camera `cam:{fy:700}` at k=1 (the ground line moves up the frame), keep captions in y 100-300, and nothing important below y 880. Short 1 still has the old bottom-heavy framing: fix it next. Full upgrade list: `NEXT_LEVEL.md`.

## Cinematic shots, not slides (user complaint 2026-11-19: "it feels like a presentation switching images")
Rules: (1) fewer, LONGER shots (10-18 s) where something keeps moving: the camera, the hero, the extras; never one static character per background. (2) Camera: `camMoves:[{t,dur,fx,fy,k,r,dx,dy,ease}]` (all optional keys) for tracking pans (`ease:"none"`, same speed as the walker), push-ins (k 1.0 -> 1.5-1.95 towards the face), roll `r`; `handheld:4` adds a finite micro-shake; `drift:false` only when camMoves exist. (3) Continuity: let the camera DISCOVER the next character (pan until Sam enters the frame), then push in; no cut needed. (4) Walkers: `walk:[t0,t1,dx]` (negative dx = walking left, side view auto); mascots with `curl:false`, `age`, `skin`, `shirt` make a varied cast (dad = `age:56,cane:false`, boss = `age:42`). Number tags follow walkers automatically and each tag reveal gives a camera punch (shake + roll jolt; `punch:1.6` stronger, `punch:false` off). (5) Captions go in `overlay` (screen-fixed SVG, not in the camera group) and are animated from `js`; show them BEFORE a tag appears so they never overlap; text slams with `S.slamIn(tl,sel,t,C)`; keep text blocks over open sky/background, not over a face. (6) **Never reuse a scene's char ids in your own element ids**: chars are `<sid>-c0`, `<sid>-c1`...; name text `<sid>-tx..`, captions `<sid>-cap..` (an id clash once hid the hero whenever that text showed). (7) Sizes in portrait: characters scale 1.6 at y 1040; tag y is computed from the head. Working example: `specs/short_trust.json` (3 shots, 45 s).

## Number tags (glowing number over a head; built 2026-11-19)
`"tags":[{"ch":"hero"|index,"text":"62","t":0.35,"to":[[2.0,"41"]],"size":130,"col":"#hex","dy":70,"x":..,"y":..}]` on any scene with chars. The number pops in with a bounce as a glowing number ONLY (white digits, thin dark outline, soft coloured glow that pulses gently, finite loop). **No pill, box or background behind it (user, 2026-11-19).** Colour is automatic: 70+ green, 45-69 amber, under 45 red, non-numbers cyan (or set `col`); each `to` step swaps the value with a new pop. Use for trust scores, prices, ages, power levels.
