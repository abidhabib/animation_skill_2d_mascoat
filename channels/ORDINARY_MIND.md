## Taste
- Editorial and premium: deep navy / graphite / warm paper, ONE accent per video; green and red only for up and down.
- Modern UI (glass panels, real number formats, live charts); no cartoon sparkles.
- Pop-ups (`popups.js`), pick up to 3: flap, odometer, receipt, dial, stamp, tape, note. Avoid burst, jar, sticker.
- Captions: `serif` or `strip`. Never `punch` unless asked.
- Music: tension / calm / dark / wonder, low; effects: ticks, paper, soft hits.
- Camera: purposeful orbit, dolly, crane, parallax; text never leaves its container (run `--overflow`).

## Facts and safety
- Verify every number at a primary source; keep a fact table in the evidence folder.
- No buy/sell calls or price targets; not-financial-advice line. Invented numbers get a small "illustrative" tag. No sources card on screen (user decision).
- No brand logos unless asked. Follow `YOUTUBE_SAFETY.md`.

## Format and packaging
- Titles are plain finance titles, never "What If": subject names first, hard fact + question, under about 60 characters.
- Thumbnails: the two subjects or one object, a 2-word text, editorial look.
