# Script template: long form 11-13 minutes (short variant at the bottom)
Narration about 1,200-1,500 words. Tag other voices `NAME (emotion): "line"` (8 words or fewer, ~10% of words). Notes in [brackets] are stripped when VO chunks are made.

Premise: <you + rule + catch>   Title: <...>   Thumbnail text (2 words, not the title): <...>   Loops promised: <list with where they pay off>

| Time | Beat | Write here (connect every beat with THEREFORE or BUT) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:40 | HOOK: concrete moment, show the power/problem twice, plant open loop #1 | <...> |
| ~0:40 | PREMISE: rule in 3 lines + a measurable test | <...> |
| 1:00-3:30 | FUN/SETUP: 4-6 quick wins with a punch word each, viewer question ~2:00, re-hook before the end | <...> |
| 3:30-6:00 | ESCALATION: bigger stage each step, a side-character touch ~every 45 s, micro-cliffhanger per step | <...> |
| ~6:00 | MIDPOINT TWIST: a rule changes or a cost appears; open the BIG loop | <...> |
| 6:00-8:30 | CONSEQUENCES: stakes up, second viewer question, pay small loops | <...> |
| 8:30-10:30 | CLIMAX: moral choice with countdown, [SILENCE 1-2s] before the line, pay the title's promise | <...> |
| 10:30-12:00 | PAYOFF + reverse twist + sequel hook + comment question; subscribe only after payoff | <...> |

Checks (aim 5/5): one-second clarity - an open loop active at every minute mark - two distinct emotions - one share-worthy line - an ending question with no single right answer.
Also: Therefore/But between beats - a re-hook at least every 90-120 s - a number or test in every beat - every twist tied to time, money, family, love, survival or status - no filler (add a test, a person affected, a harder consequence).
Production: scenes go in `specs/clipN.json` with `say.at` phrases (LIBRARY.md); use `age` for Day-N ageing.

## Short variant (7-9 min)
Hook 0:00-0:35 / rules 0:35-1:10 / perks 1:10-2:30 / escalation 2:30-4:00 / consequences 4:00-4:30 / cost 4:30-5:00 / climax 5:00-6:30 / payoff 6:30-7:00 / sequel + question 7:00-7:30.
