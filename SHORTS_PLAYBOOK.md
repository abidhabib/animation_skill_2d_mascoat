# Shorts playbook (both channels), added 2026-11-26

Source: a free creator doc the user shared ("Zero View Jail", a promo for a paid tool). Summarised in our words.
Its numbers and claims about YouTube's system are **one creator's advice, not verified**: treat them as things to test and judge by our own Studio data.

## 1. Read the numbers first
| Studio shows | Likely meaning | Look at |
|---|---|---|
| a few hundred to a couple thousand views, then flat | shown to a test group, did not hold them | idea, length, first 2 seconds |
| 0-3 views for 48 h | possibly an account problem, not the video | section 5 |

## 2. Idea (not from a blank AI prompt)
1. Collect 3 real recent outliers from a NEIGHBOURING niche (views at least about 10x subscribers, last 7 days) with their transcripts.
2. Study only them: first 1.5 s, structure, pacing, why a stranger stayed. Take the psychology, never the topic, script, characters or art.
3. Offer ideas none of the 3 covered, avoiding the stock AI topics (everyone vanishes, never sleep, famous disasters).
   Ask the model for: title, spoken first sentence, one line on why it could beat the channel's usual views.

## 3. Script in the user's own voice
Ask the user for a 45 s voice memo (messy is fine). Build the script from THEIR words, slang and jokes: reorder and cut repeats only.
If no memo is given, say so and write it, but mark it as AI-drafted.

## 4. The Short itself
- Length **38-55 s**. (15 s clips flatter retention percent but hold less time.)
- First word at 0 s, no dead air; camera or character already moving on frame 1.
- **Hook title on frame 1**: 2 lines, centre-top safe area, 2 emotional words in a strong colour: `hook()` in `popups.js`.
- Something new about **every 7 s**, preferably a "but..." that raises the stakes.
- Run `python "_Studio Kit/growkaz/shortcheck.py" <project folder>` before sending any Short.

## 5. Upload and account (user does these)
- Google account that is aged, or warmed up for about 48 h of normal use.
- Studio > Settings > Channel > Feature eligibility: first two green, phone verified.
- At most 2 Shorts a day, steadily for 21+ days. Never a dump of many at once.
- Choose the most dramatic frame as the Short's thumbnail (phone pencil icon).

## Our own limits (win over the source)
- "Clone a channel's look" means measure technique only (pace, framing, caption placement). Never copy another creator's characters, art, audio or style identity; see `YOUTUBE_SAFETY.md`.
- Thinkle Show uses "What If" framing, which the source calls saturated: only use it with an original premise (search YouTube first) or test a plain title against it.
- Ordinary Mind keeps verified facts and no advice.
